# Payments

## Payment Methods

We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 rupees. We also accept ShopEase gift cards and wallet balance.

## EMI and Instalments

You can pay in monthly instalments (EMI). No-cost EMI is available on credit card purchases above 3,000 rupees for tenures of 3 or 6 months. Standard EMI with interest is available for 9 and 12 months. Debit card EMI is available for selected banks.

## Failed Payments and Double Charges

If a payment fails but money is deducted from your account, the amount is automatically reversed by your bank within 7 business days. If you were charged twice for the same order, the duplicate amount is refunded within 5 business days once our payments team confirms it.

## Coupons and Offers

Only one coupon can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be combined with other offers and are not valid on gift cards. If you return part of an order that used a coupon, the refund is adjusted by the coupon value for that item.

## Invoices and GST

A GST invoice is emailed when your order ships and can be downloaded any time from My Account, Orders. To get a business invoice with your company's GSTIN, add the GSTIN at checkout before placing the order. Invoices cannot be changed after the order ships.

## Payment Security

All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways. ShopEase never stores your full card number or CVV. We will never ask for your OTP, PIN or password by phone or email.
