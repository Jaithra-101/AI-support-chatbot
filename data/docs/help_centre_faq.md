# Help Centre FAQ

## Do you price match?
We do not price match other websites or stores. We run new deals every week, and ShopEase Plus members get 5 percent extra off on electronics.

## Do you have a loyalty or rewards program?
Yes. Every purchase earns ShopEase coins, 1 coin for every 100 rupees spent. 100 coins are worth 50 rupees off your next order. Coins expire after 12 months.

## Can I buy a gift card?
Yes. Digital gift cards from 500 to 10,000 rupees can be bought on the Gift Cards page and are emailed instantly. Gift cards are valid for 12 months and cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.

## Can I send an order as a gift?
Yes. Choose Gift wrap at checkout for 49 rupees. The invoice is not included in gift-wrapped parcels, and you can add a free personal message.

## Do you sell refurbished products?
No. Everything sold on ShopEase is brand new and sealed.

## Can I order in bulk for my company?
Yes. For orders of more than 20 units, email bulk@shopease.example for a quote with business pricing and a GST invoice.

## Do you have physical stores?
No. ShopEase is an online-only store, but you can collect orders from over 400 partner pickup points.

## Can I pick up my order instead of delivery?
Yes. Choose a partner pickup point at checkout. Your order is held there for 7 days and you need to show the order OTP to collect it.

## Is cash on delivery available everywhere?
Cash on delivery is available on most pin codes for orders up to 10,000 rupees. It is not available for gift cards or ShopEase Plus.

## Why was my order cancelled automatically?
Orders are cancelled automatically if the payment is not completed within 30 minutes, if the item goes out of stock, or if the address is not serviceable. Any amount paid is refunded within 5 to 7 business days.

## What if an item is out of stock?
Tap Notify Me on the product page and we will email you when it is back in stock. Restocks usually take 1 to 3 weeks.

## Can I reserve a product?
No, products cannot be reserved. Stock is only held once an order is paid.

## Do you charge a fee for cash on delivery?
No. Cash on delivery is free.

## Are prices inclusive of taxes?
Yes. All prices shown include GST. Shipping fees, if any, are shown at checkout.

## Can I get a discount on my first order?
Yes. New customers get 10 percent off their first order up to 200 rupees with the code WELCOME10.

## How do I apply a coupon?
Enter the coupon code on the checkout page and tap Apply. Only one coupon can be used per order.

## Is it safe to save my card?
Yes. Saved cards are tokenised by your bank as required by RBI rules, so ShopEase never stores your actual card number.

## Do you offer installation?
Free installation is included for large appliances and is scheduled within 2 days of delivery. Small electronics do not need installation.

## Can I change my phone number or email?
Yes. Go to My Account, Profile and verify the new phone number or email with a one-time code.

## Why did I not receive the OTP?
OTPs can take up to 2 minutes to arrive. Check that your number is correct and that DND is not blocking promotional senders. You can request a new OTP after 60 seconds.

## How do I unsubscribe from emails?
Click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any marketing email, or turn off offers under My Account, Notifications. Order updates will still be sent.

## Do you deliver on Sundays?
Standard and express deliveries are made Monday to Saturday. Same day delivery orders placed on Sunday are delivered the same day in supported cities.

## Can I change the delivery date?
For scheduled deliveries you can change the slot from My Account, Orders until the day before delivery. Standard deliveries cannot be rescheduled, but the courier will try three times.

## What if I am not home for the delivery?
The courier will call before delivering and try up to three times. You can also ask a neighbour or family member to collect it by sharing the delivery OTP.

## Can I return a gift without the buyer knowing?
Yes. The person who received a gift can return it for store credit using the order number on the gift slip. The buyer is not told about the return.

## Do products come with a bill?
Yes. A GST invoice is included in every parcel except gift-wrapped orders, and it is always available in My Account.

## How do I contact customer care?
You can chat here, email support@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567 toll free, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM.

## Do you have an app?
Yes. The ShopEase app is available on Android and iOS and offers app-only deals.

## How do I write a product review?
Open My Account, Orders, choose the delivered item and tap Write a Review. Only verified buyers can review products.

## Is my personal data safe?
Yes. Your data is encrypted and only used to process orders and provide support. We never sell your data.

## How many days do I have to return an item?
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are unused and in the original packaging. Sale items can be returned within 15 days for store credit, and damaged items must be reported within 48 hours.

## Can I return a product if I don't like it?
Yes. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, even if you simply changed your mind, as long as they are unused and in the original packaging. Sale items can be returned within 15 days for store credit.

## What if my order arrived damaged or broken? | Can I return a product that arrived damaged?
Report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Account by choosing Report a Problem and uploading photos. You get a free replacement or a full refund, with free pickup, even for sale items.

## Do you offer student discounts?
Not at the moment. New customers get 10 percent off their first order with the code WELCOME10, and we run new deals every week.

## How do I get a GST invoice with my company details?
Add your company's GSTIN at checkout before placing the order, and the GST invoice will show your business details. Invoices cannot be changed after the order ships.

## How do I make a warranty claim?
Upload a photo of the invoice and a short description of the fault on the Warranty Claims page. Our team replies within 2 business days and collects the product for repair or replacement.

## What if I damaged the product myself? | What if I broke it? | I dropped it and it broke, what can I do? | Is accidental damage covered?
Damage you caused yourself, such as drops, spills or water damage, is not covered by returns or the standard 12-month warranty. If you bought ShopEase Care, it covers one accidental damage repair per year. Otherwise you can request a paid repair from the Warranty Claims page, and our team will send you a quote within 2 business days.
