# Warranty

## Warranty

All electronics sold by ShopEase come with a 12 month manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.

## Extended Warranty

You can extend the warranty by an additional 12 months by purchasing ShopEase Care within 30 days of delivery. ShopEase Care also covers one accidental damage repair per year.

## Making a Warranty Claim

To make a warranty claim, upload a photo of the invoice and a short description of the fault from the Warranty Claims page. Our team replies within 2 business days. If the product can be repaired, we collect it and return it within 10 to 14 days. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model.
