# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders above 499 rupees and usually takes 4 to 6 business days. Orders below 499 rupees have a shipping fee of 49 rupees. Express shipping costs 149 rupees and delivers within 1 to 2 business days in metro cities. Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day.

## Delivery Areas

We deliver to over 19,000 pin codes across India. You can check whether your pin code is serviceable on any product page before ordering. We currently do not ship outside India. Delivery to remote areas in the North East, Jammu and Kashmir and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands may take 3 to 5 extra days.

## Same Day and Scheduled Delivery

Same day delivery is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad for orders placed before 11 AM, for a fee of 199 rupees. You can also choose a scheduled delivery slot at checkout for large items such as furniture and appliances.

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships you receive an SMS and email with the courier name and tracking number. You can also track every order under My Account, Orders. Our couriers are BlueDart, Delhivery and Ekart.

## Missed or Late Deliveries

If the courier cannot reach you, they will try two more times on the following business days before returning the parcel to us. If a parcel is marked as delivered but you have not received it, contact support within 48 hours so we can open an investigation with the courier. If an order is more than 5 days late, contact us and we will refund the shipping fee.
