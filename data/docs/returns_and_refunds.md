# Returns and Refunds

## Returns and Refunds

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order receipt. Items bought during a sale can be returned within 15 days of delivery, but they are eligible for store credit only, not a cash refund.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

Personalised items, gift cards, perishable goods, innerwear and opened software cannot be returned. Products with a broken seal or missing accessories are also not accepted, unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Damaged, Defective or Wrong Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Go to My Account, open the order, choose Report a Problem and upload two or three photos of the item and the packaging. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund. The return pickup is free, and this applies to sale items as well, which are refunded in full rather than as store credit. If the outer box looks damaged when the courier arrives, you may refuse the delivery and we will ship a replacement.

## How to Start a Return

To start a return, open My Account, choose the order and select Request Return. Pick a reason and a pickup slot. A courier will collect the parcel within 3 business days. Please keep the item packed in its original box with all tags and accessories.

## Refund Timelines

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. UPI and card refunds usually appear within 5 business days, net banking within 7 business days. For cash on delivery orders, the refund is sent to your bank account by transfer once you add your bank details. Store credit is added to your ShopEase wallet within 24 hours.

## Exchanges

Clothing and shoes can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery, as long as the new size is in stock. Exchanges are free. Other products cannot be exchanged directly; return the item and place a new order instead.
