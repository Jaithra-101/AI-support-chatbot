# Orders and Account

## Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order free of charge any time before it is dispatched. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or request a return after it arrives.

## Changing an Order

Items in an order cannot be changed after it is placed. If the order has not shipped, cancel it and place a new order. The delivery address can be changed until the order is dispatched.

## Your Account

You can sign up with your email address or mobile number. Under My Account you can update your profile, saved addresses, payment methods and notification settings. To reset your password, choose Forgot password on the login page and follow the link sent to your email, which is valid for 30 minutes.

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account from My Account, Settings. Your personal data is removed within 30 days, except invoices, which we must keep for 8 years by law. Open orders must be delivered or cancelled before the account can be deleted.

## Privacy

We only use your personal data to process orders, provide support and, if you agree, send offers. We never sell your data to third parties. You can download a copy of your data or opt out of marketing emails from My Account, Privacy.

## ShopEase Plus Membership

ShopEase Plus costs 999 rupees a year and gives free express shipping on every order, early access to sales and 5 percent extra off on electronics. Membership can be cancelled within 14 days for a full refund.
